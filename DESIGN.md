# Design

Approved direction: dark musical glassmorphism. Operate mode for dashboard, Persuade mode for welcome. Designly composition principles: one dominant artist feature; secondary song and artist lists recede.

Charcoal #101515, smoked glass rgba(28,36,35,.82), text #f2f5ee, secondary #afbab4, action green #b4efb7. Manrope headings, DM Sans interface, self-hosted Latin fonts. Main panels and navigation get translucent surfaces, thin edges and moderate blur; individual list rows remain flat. 16px panel corners, 24px primary spacing.

Desktop sidebar becomes a compact mobile navigation row. Preserve keyboard focus, skip navigation, reduced motion and opaque fallback. Rankings are affinity, never listening counts. Demo provenance remains visible. Original vector covers provide musical atmosphere without pretending to represent Spotify artists.

Brand: Mood.i. The approved Onda symbol is implemented as crisp native SVG with a rounded wave M and separate dot. Use the shared Brand component for consistent navigation wordmarks. The generated charcoal concept is retained in public/brand/moodi-onda.png as the visual reference.

Wordmark typography: locally served Comfortaa 700, selected to approximate the rounded lettering of the approved generated Onda concept.

Motion: one shared galactic depth field persists across routes. Scroll moves nebulae, star dust and orbital geometry at distinct bounded speeds; pointer position refracts light on smoked glass. Panels arrive once as a glass plane, controls respond in under 220ms. Content remains visible without motion code. No animation dependency; passive listeners coalesce into requestAnimationFrame, IntersectionObserver handles entrances, hidden tabs pause ambient twinkling and reduced motion removes spatial effects.
