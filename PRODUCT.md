# Product

Mood.i is a Spanish-language portfolio dashboard for music listeners and recruiters evaluating frontend craft. The public demo must work without a Spotify account. Success: explore rankings, switch periods, and understand data provenance on mobile and desktop.

V1 includes welcome, overview, top tracks, top artists, recent activity, OAuth callback, and project explanation. Demo names and vector artwork are fictional. Spotify integration is read-only and uses PKCE with session-only token storage. No backend, playback, playlist writes, genres, listening totals, or fabricated historical trends.
